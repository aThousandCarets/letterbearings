    How to play

    Guess the next letter from the available choices. A miss narrows the range. A hit locks in the letter and updates the choices.

    ←
    EarlierThe answer letter comes before your guess.
    →
    LaterThe answer letter comes after your guess.
    ✓
    CorrectThe letter locks into place.
    ✦
    Appears laterYour guess is also somewhere further in the word.
    Correct letter or word0 points
    Wrong letter+1 point
    Wrong word+1, plus each typed letter from the first miss

    Normal shows the word length. Prestige has a different word and keeps its length hidden during play.
